Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Trivox Games Studio ("we", "us", or "our") manages user information across our published mobile games on Google Play.

1. Information Collection and Use

Our games are built for broad audience entertainment. We do not require users to register or provide personal identity data to play our offline or single-player games. We do not harvest sensitive data, phone numbers, or private user files.

2. Third-Party Integrations

To support basic diagnostics and core game engine services, our apps may interface with standard Google Play Services (e.g., Google Play Games Services, Firebase Crashlytics). These services collect anonymous diagnostics, device models, and crash stacks strictly for operational debugging.

3. Advertising

If advertisements are displayed, they are served through certified Google AdMob ad networks in strict accordance with the Google Play Developer Policy. Ad networks may utilize unique, resettable advertising IDs to serve relevant ads.

4. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If any game is intended for family audiences, we restrict third-party SDKs to child-directed mode in compliance with COPPA.

5. Inquiries & Contact

For questions or privacy requests regarding our applications, email us at: trivoxgames@gmail.com.
