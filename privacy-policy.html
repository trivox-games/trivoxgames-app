Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile games and applications developed and published by Trivox Games Studio ("we", "us", or "our") on the Google Play Store. We are committed to transparency and protecting user privacy.

1. Information Collection and Storage

Our mobile titles are designed to provide entertainment without requiring personal user registration. We do not collect, harvest, store, or sell any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your personal name, physical address, email address, or phone number.

2. Third-Party Service Providers

To maintain game reliability, identify hardware compatibility issues, and provide baseline analytics, our applications may utilize trusted third-party SDKs provided by Google. These services may collect non-personally identifiable diagnostic identifiers (such as device model, OS version, and crash telemetry):

  • Google Play Services: Core system connectivity and game services.
  • Google AdMob: Serving child-safe, policy-compliant contextual or interest-based ads.
  • Firebase Crashlytics / Diagnostics: Anonymous crash and performance monitoring.

For further details on how Google processes telemetry data, please review the official Google Privacy & Terms.

3. Advertising Standards

Advertisements displayed within our applications strictly adhere to the Google Play Developer Distribution Agreement and Families Policy (where applicable). Advertising IDs utilized by ad networks are resettable through your Android device settings.

4. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)

We take child safety seriously. If an application is targeted toward children or families, third-party advertising SDKs are automatically configured to run in restricted mode (no targeted advertising or persistent cross-app tracking), fully complying with COPPA regulations.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because our studio does not maintain remote user databases or collect personal accounts, we do not store personal records to delete. Anonymous crash analytics gathered via Google services are automatically cleared per Google's standard data retention schedules.
